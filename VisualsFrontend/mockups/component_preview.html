GOLDEN RULE #1 (Oxford Guide Ch 2)

Subject-Verb Agreement with Indefinite Pronouns

Words such as each, neither, either, and everyone take a singular verb in standard formal English.

📝 Tip: "Neither of the boys was (not were) present."
